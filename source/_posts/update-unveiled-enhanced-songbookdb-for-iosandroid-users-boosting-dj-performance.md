## Update Unveiled: Enhanced SongbookDB for iOS/Android Users, Boosting DJ Performance

[![online song book songbookdb update 1.5.6](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/songbookdbappupdate-coverimage.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/songbookdbappupdate-coverimage.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "online song book songbookdb update 1.5.6")

The **[SongbookDB](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** singer app, used to browse, search and request songs, has been updated to version 1.5.6\. The **free maintenance update** is available for both Android and iOS.

#### Here’s what’s new and improved:

* Set up auto-pagination, so when singers get to the bottom of search results the next 50 results are auto-loaded
* Fixed some UI features that appeared out of place or off-center on larger mobile screens
* Updated Facebook graph to 2.6

The SongbookDB singers app in conjunction with your [SongbookDB subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) for use with PCDJ Karaoki **enables your karaoke singers to browse, search and request tracks with key changes and personal notes**. Singer **song requests come in via the internet** directly to the SongbookDB plug-in for Karaoki. Simply click ‘accept’ to send the singers name and song request to Karaoki’s rotation list.

This modern, **mobile song book** request method truly makes printed song books seem archaic and messy – and adds additional flair to your shows. The ability to **buzz a singers phone when it’s their turn to sing, send a shout-out across all singers viewing the song book at once and other included bonus features** makes the SongbookDB with Karaoki the most robust and intuitive online singer/song request system available.
