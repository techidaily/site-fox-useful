## Save the Fun: Seamlessly Transferring Songbooks From PartyTyme Karaoke to PC via PDF Export Techniques

[![Exporting a Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription Songbook](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/partytymekaraokesongbook-coverimage.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/partytymekaraokesongbook-coverimage.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "Exporting a Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription Songbook")

New in [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [LYRX](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com) is [Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) support, providing in-app access to a commercially legal catalog of HD quality karaoke songs.

One great subscriber benefit of the [Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) service is the ability to export a PDF karaoke song book directly from the Party Tyme website. The songbook is updated in real-time so as soon as new songs are available in the subscription they are available in the downloadable (and printable) book.

All subscribers to the Party Tyme Karaoke subscription service need to do is log into the [www.partytymeplayer.com](http://www.partytymeplayer.com) website using the same user name and password credentials used to log into the service in [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) or [LYRX](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com/) karaoke software, and the tools to create a (somewhat) customized karaoke songbook are readily available.

3) Once you’re logged in, locate the ‘**GEAR**‘ looking icon in top right corner of the website and click on it. This will bring up initial settings for the PT karaoke songbook.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Click open under other options](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/click-open-pt.jpg?fit=300%2C300&ssl=1 "Click open under other options")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/click-open-pt.jpg?fit=780%2C780&ssl=1)

4) On the settings screen, under ‘**Other Options**‘, click the **OPEN** button. This will launch the ‘**Export To PDF**‘ settings and creation menu.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Select your songbook options and click OK](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/click-ok-pt.jpg?fit=300%2C300&ssl=1 "Select your songbook options and click OK")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/click-ok-pt.jpg?fit=652%2C652&ssl=1)

After dialing in your options, click the **OK** button. After clicking on **OK** the songbook creation process will begin.
